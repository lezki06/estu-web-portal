About Eskişehir Technical University

Established by Law No. 7141 on May 18, 2018, Eskişehir Technical University (ESTÜ) is situated at the heart of Eskişehir—a city renowned for its vibrant youth culture, science, and arts. Operating across three different campuses, our university serves as a hub for innovation and dynamic education. ESTÜ comprises 6 Faculties, 3 Vocational Schools, 1 School, 3 Institutes, and 8 Research and Application Centers. With a robust infrastructure and a faculty of experts dedicated to global developments, we prepare our students to be well-equipped, competitive individuals in creative learning environments.

A unique feature of our institution is the Hasan Polatkan International Airport located on the 2 Eylül Campus. Remarkably, this airport is managed directly by our Faculty of Aeronautics and Astronautics, hosting various international flights throughout the week.
