Campus Life

🍽 Dining & Cafeterias

  • Main cafeteria – serves two meals a day, lunch and dinner.
  • Engineering Faculty canteen – sandwiches, snacks, coffee
  • Science Faculty café – light meals and beverages
  • Subsidized meal cards available for all students

📅 Annual Events

  • Spring Festival – Usually held in May, featuring concerts, student club stands, and outdoor sports activities at the 2 Eylül Campus.
  • Aviation Days – May/June: An iconic event for ESTÜ where model aircraft shows and aviation-themed workshops take place at the Hasan Polatkan Airport area.
  • ESTU Culture and Art Days – May: A week-long celebration with theater plays, international food stalls, and music performances by student communities.
  • Graduation Ceremony – June: Held at the ESTÜ Stadium (2 Eylül Campus), celebrating the success of thousands of graduates.
